Gensokyo Harvest (working title)

Touhou doujin · farming × roguelite × rhythm combat · built in Unity

The pitch

A small Touhou Project fan game where you tend a cozy farm in Gensokyo by day, Stardew Valley-style, and dive into roguelite runs where every fight follows the beat, like Crypt of the NecroDancer: grid-based moves, one action per beat. Inspiration and a similar in-development project: Freaked Fleapit.

Premise

The player steps through one of Yukari's boundaries and ends up in Gensokyo, near the Human Village. By chance they meet Marisa Kirisame, who takes an interest in them and hands over a way to study magic (an artifact or a tome), charging them a hefty price for it.

The game starts at the beginning of spring, with a small plot of land for growing crops and selling vegetables.

Setting

Gensokyo, just before the Touhou 7 incident (Perfect Cherry Blossom, the spring that never comes).

ZUN's source material is used lightly. The story is secondary to the gameplay.
